8Bit-CPU control ROM
====================

Address: A7-A4 = opcode (IR0 bits 7-4), A3-A1 = step counter, A0 = zero flag
Chip 0: D0-D7 = PC_OUT RAM_OUT IR1_OUT REG_OUT ALU_OUT IN_OUT PC_INC HLT
Chip 1: D0-D7 = MAR_LD RAM_WE IR0_LD IR1_LD A_LD B_LD REG_LD OUT_LD
Chip 2: D0-D7 = PC_LD Z_LD END ALU_OP0 ALU_OP1 ALU_OP2 REG_SEL0 REG_SEL1
24 control lines in total.

Fetch (every instruction, steps 0-3)
  step 0: PC_OUT MAR_LD
  step 1: RAM_OUT PC_INC IR0_LD
  step 2: PC_OUT MAR_LD
  step 3: RAM_OUT PC_INC IR1_LD

nop (opcode 0x0)
  step 4: END

add (opcode 0x1)
  step 4: REG_OUT A_LD REG_SEL=x
  step 5: REG_OUT B_LD REG_SEL=y
  step 6: ALU_OUT REG_LD END REG_SEL=c ALU=add

sub (opcode 0x2)
  step 4: REG_OUT A_LD REG_SEL=x
  step 5: REG_OUT B_LD REG_SEL=y
  step 6: ALU_OUT REG_LD END REG_SEL=c ALU=sub

and (opcode 0x3)
  step 4: REG_OUT A_LD REG_SEL=x
  step 5: REG_OUT B_LD REG_SEL=y
  step 6: ALU_OUT REG_LD END REG_SEL=c ALU=and

or (opcode 0x4)
  step 4: REG_OUT A_LD REG_SEL=x
  step 5: REG_OUT B_LD REG_SEL=y
  step 6: ALU_OUT REG_LD END REG_SEL=c ALU=or

xor (opcode 0x5)
  step 4: REG_OUT A_LD REG_SEL=x
  step 5: REG_OUT B_LD REG_SEL=y
  step 6: ALU_OUT REG_LD END REG_SEL=c ALU=xor

ldi (opcode 0x6)
  step 4: IR1_OUT REG_LD END REG_SEL=x

ld (opcode 0x7)
  step 4: IR1_OUT MAR_LD
  step 5: RAM_OUT REG_LD END REG_SEL=x

st (opcode 0x8)
  step 4: IR1_OUT MAR_LD
  step 5: REG_OUT RAM_WE END REG_SEL=x

out (opcode 0x9)
  step 4: REG_OUT OUT_LD END REG_SEL=x

in (opcode 0xa)
  step 4: IN_OUT REG_LD END REG_SEL=x

jmp (opcode 0xb)
  step 4: IR1_OUT PC_LD END

jz (opcode 0xc)
  step 4: REG_OUT Z_LD REG_SEL=x
  step 5 (zero flag clear): END
  step 5 (zero flag set):   IR1_OUT PC_LD END

hlt (opcode 0xf)
  step 4: HLT

Unused opcodes (0xd, 0xe)
  step 4: HLT
